    <h1 class="cc-display cc-h1">About</h1>
    <p class="cc-sub">an initiative of Life Itself and friends</p>
    <div class="cc-col" style="margin-top:1.5rem">
      <p>Conscious Communities is the home of the developmental spaces field: resources and information on developmental spaces, and the meeting point for a growing <a class="cc-lnk" href="/network">network of organisations</a> working in it.</p>
      <p>It is part of a <a class="cc-lnk" href="https://lifeitself.org/blog/2023/06/20/building-field-for-developmental-spaces">wider effort at Life Itself</a>, together with Fetzer, Ekskäret, Commonweal, Springboard and LIONE, to gather what is known and build a field for developmental spaces.</p>

<section class="cc-wrap cc-sec">
  <h2 class="cc-display cc-h2">Two names</h2>
  <div class="cc-col" style="margin-top:1rem">
    <p><em>Conscious communities</em> is what most people call the places this site is about. <em>Developmental spaces</em> is the name the <a class="cc-lnk" href="/paper">whitepaper</a> gives the concept: dedicated, growth-oriented spaces where communities engage in sustained, multi-domain inner development. The site uses both. The front door speaks the first language; the concept, the paper, the manifesto and the network keep the second.</p>
  </div>
</section>
