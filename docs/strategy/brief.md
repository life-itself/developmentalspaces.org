## Mission

A world with many more **developmental spaces**: communities, schools, retreat centres — even new kinds of monastery or university — dedicated to cultivating the inner capacities needed for a paradigmatic (second renaissance) transition.

## What the site is

The single home for this work (absorbing consciouscoliving.org and tealestate.net; archipelago.place stays separate, cross-linked). It is an **active publishing library**: whitepaper + manifesto as credibility spine, self-running email courses, a Substack, and a growing stream of published material. It is *not* (currently) a shopfront for hosted offerings.

## Positioning line

> The home of the developmental spaces field: learn about conscious community living, build developmental spaces, and fund them.

## Audiences & job stories

Front door: **people exploring conscious communities and developmental spaces** — from curious to seriously considering joining or building (validated by Feb 2026 community consultation).

| Stream | Primary user | Job story |
|---|---|---|
| **Home** | Anyone arriving | "When I land here, I want to grasp in 30 seconds what this is and which path is mine, so I can go one level deeper." |
| **Learn** | Curious → considering joining (the curious-to-considering persona from the consultation) | "When I'm drawn to living more consciously in community, I want plain-language intros and a guided course, so I can decide if and how to take a step." |
| **Build** | Founders, designers of developmental environments, field-builders, researchers, funders-of-the-field (the founder, designer and field-builder personas from the consultation) | "When I'm creating a space or building this field, I want the canonical concept (paper/manifesto), the network, and design knowledge, so I can build well and credibly." |
| **Fund** | Investors, capital partners, land stewards; projects raising | "When I want to put capital into (or raise capital for) these spaces, I want the investment thesis and live deal-flow, so I can act." |

Secondary rule: one person often spans streams (therapist who colives; founder who invests). That's why nav is by **function, not audience**.

## Voice & register

- **Home + Learn**: plain, warm, concrete. No jargon on first contact ("communities where people grow" not "multi-domain ontological development").
- **Build**: the manifesto/whitepaper register lives here — visionary, rigorous, institutional. One click deep, never on the front door.
- **Fund**: sober, credible, finance-literate but values-explicit.
- Never hard-sell (the old CoCo copy leaned salesy — soften on migration; done for /learn).
