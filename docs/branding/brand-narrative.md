Migrated on 2026-09-18 from `../devspaces-consolidation/brand-narrative.md`, where it was ratified on 2026-07-12. Revised for the naming flip of 2026-09-18. ## Decision 1: Posture — how strong do we go?

| | A. Reference point | B. Publishing home ← **decided** | C. Movement platform |
|---|---|---|---|
| What it is | Minimalist landing: the idea, the paper, the network list (the Sep 2025 framing) | Active library + newsletter: streams, courses, archive, steady publishing of the backlog, deal-flow updates | Full network hub: member directory, events, community features, gatherings |
| Cadence | Touch rarely | Publish when there is something (weekly to monthly) | Requires community management |
| Capacity needed | Minimal | What we already do (Substack + occasional pages) | More than currently exists |
| Risk | Wastes the merged content; consolidation pointless | Ambition creep toward C | Empty-restaurant problem |

**Decision: B, explicitly not C (yet).** The consolidation built B; the backlog feeds it; it needs no new capacity beyond writing and publishing. C stays a future option the narrative can gesture at ("a developing network") without promising. This deliberately supersedes the Sep 2025 "minimalist landing" framing.

## Decision 2: Audience — who is this most for?

The evidence (WhatsApp consultation, fund job stories, who actually shows up) gives a primary plus two secondary audiences rather than one:

1. **Primary: the seriously curious.** People exploring conscious community living: considering joining, visiting, or starting. They arrive via coliving language, courses, search. They are the volume audience and the top of every funnel. (Validated: Carolyn, Simon, the course subscribers.)
2. **Secondary 1: builders and designers.** Founders of spaces, designers of developmental environments (An, ~R, Phillip). They need the paper, design knowledge, the network.
3. **Secondary 2: capital and field.** Investors, funders, partner organisations (Fetzer, Ekskäret et al.). Fewest in number, largest per-capita impact. They need credibility artifacts: paper, manifesto, partner logos, fund thinking.

The front door speaks to 1 in plain language; 2 and 3 must recognise themselves within one click (Build / Fund). Learn / Build / Fund implements this; this doc makes it doctrine.

One person often spans streams (a therapist who colives; a founder who invests). That is why navigation is by function, not by audience.

## Decision 3: The narrative

| | |
|---|---|
| **Our ambition** | A world where living in conscious community, in spaces devoted to growing together, is easy to find, real to experience, and sustainable to build; where such developmental spaces are as normal a life choice as a university or a neighbourhood. |
| **Why we are here** | The crises of our time are, at root, crises of inner development, and modernity has no institutions for it. People increasingly want this way of living; pioneers are building spaces for it; capital is looking for it. But the three cannot find each other, and the knowledge is scattered. |
| **What and how** | The home of the developmental spaces field. We help people **discover** conscious community living (courses, guides), help pioneers **build** spaces (whitepaper, manifesto, network, design knowledge), and help projects get **funded** (capital thinking, deal-flow). We publish openly and steadily: a working library, not a brochure. |
| **What makes us different** | We bridge registers the way we bridge worlds: rigorous *and* warm. A research-grade whitepaper next to a friendly email course; a manifesto with institutional signatories next to honest archives of what we tried and what did not launch. Radical transparency (we publish our archives) plus ten-plus years of lived practice (Life Itself hubs, residencies, courses). |
| **Who we are here for** | First: people seriously exploring conscious community living. Behind them: those building such spaces, and those funding them. |
| **Ownership and voice** | Stewarded by Life Itself, held with partners: "Life Itself-powered, part of a wider ecosystem". Distributed stewardship, not a house brand. |
| **Naming** | Dual-name structure, decided 2026-09-18 (see [naming.md](naming.md)): **Conscious Communities** is the public name and masthead. **Developmental Spaces** names the concept, the whitepaper, the manifesto, the network and the Build stream. Pattern: Fundamental Wellbeing / PNSE; Second Renaissance / Pragmatic Utopianism. |
| **Values** | Growth, honesty (including about failures), patience, practicality, service to the field over the brand. |
| **Personality** | Warm-rigorous. Visionary but concrete. Never salesy, never vague. |

## The wide-door / strong-spine doctrine

Ratified 2026-07-12. Resolves the strong-versus-gentle tension permanently. Unchanged by the naming flip; if anything, the flip is the doctrine applied more consistently.

- The strong-versus-gentle question is an architecture, not a choice. The spine (the Developmental Spaces thesis, the paradigmatic-transition argument, the whitepaper voice) stays at full strength. It is the moat against generic "conscious living" content, and it is carried by artifacts no lifestyle brand has: a research-grade paper, institutional signatories, published archives.
- The door stays wide: "conscious communities" does the entry work in the masthead, tagline, Learn stream, courses and search. The intended visitor experience is two-beat: "this is about the kind of community I want to live in" → "oh, there is serious design thinking here."
- Strong language is never deleted, only placed: front door = human; About, manifesto and paper = full civilisational voice, one click deep.

## Voice and register by stream

From BRIEF.md (2026-07-11).

- **Home and Learn**: plain, warm, concrete. No jargon on first contact ("communities where people grow", not "multi-domain ontological development").
- **Build**: the manifesto and whitepaper register lives here: visionary, rigorous, institutional. One click deep, never on the front door.
- **Fund**: sober, credible, finance-literate but values-explicit.
- Never hard-sell. The old consciouscoliving.org copy leaned salesy; soften on migration (done for Learn).

## Positioning line

> The home of the developmental spaces field: learn about conscious community living, build developmental spaces, and fund them.

With the naming flip, a front-door version: *Conscious Communities: the home for people exploring, building and funding developmental spaces.*
