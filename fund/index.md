    <p class="cc-cap">The problem</p>
    <h2 class="cc-display cc-h2" style="margin-top:.5rem;max-width:22ch">Initiatives lack capital. Investors lack initiatives.</h2>
    <div class="cc-col" style="margin-top:1.25rem"><p>Community-scale properties need substantial capital; groups pool money slowly and borrow badly; and the people best suited to this life are rarely the highest earners. Meanwhile investors who want a stable, ethical home for capital cannot find vetted projects. Bridging that gap, bringing confidence to capital and capital to conscious communities, is what this stream is about.</p><p><a class="cc-lnk" href="/fund/archive/scqh">The full analysis</a></p></div>

<section class="cc-wrap cc-sec">
  <h2 class="cc-display cc-h2">From fund to platform</h2>
  <p class="cc-col" style="margin-top:1rem">Between 2019 and 2021 we designed a real-estate fund for conscious community. It did not launch, for reasons of capacity rather than conviction, and the thinking became something lighter: a matching point between projects seeking capital and people who want to invest. That deal-flow is now emerging, and we publish it here.</p>
