    <h1 class="cc-display cc-h1" style="font-size:clamp(2.2rem,4.6vw,3.6rem)">The Fund Archive (2019–2021)</h1>
    <div class="cc-col" style="margin-top:1.5rem">
      <p>Between 2019 and 2021, together with collaborators, we did serious design work on a real-estate fund for conscious community — thesis, prospectus, brand, project-evaluation process, investor conversations. The fund didn't launch (<a class="cc-lnk" href="/fund/the-fund-we-almost-built">here's that story</a>), but the thinking remains substantial and useful — to us, and we hope to anyone working on capital for community. So we're publishing it as an open archive.</p>
      <p><strong>A note on names.</strong> The project went through several working names as the thinking evolved: <em>Wise Living Fund</em> (2019) → <em>Mindful Living Fund</em> and <em>True Home Fund</em> (2020) → <em>Conscious Community Capital</em> (2021). Documents retain the name of their era.</p>
