    <div class="cc-prose">
      <h2 class="cc-h2">What we mean by a developmental space</h2>
      <p style="margin-top:1.25rem">A place dedicated to growth, where a community practises inner development across several domains at once: how we think, how awake we are, what we have healed, how wide our care extends, and what we do about it. Not a retreat you visit for a week. A life you live with others, for years.</p>
      <p>Some are co-living houses. Some are retreat centres, schools, or something like a monastery for people who are not monks. The <a href="/paper">whitepaper</a> calls them developmental spaces. Most people call them conscious communities. Both names are right, and this site uses both.</p>
      <p class="cc-caption">The spiral is from the whitepaper cover, drawn in pencil. Growth circles and returns rather than climbing in a straight line.</p>
    </div>

<!-- why now: the one dark block -->
<section class="cc-spine cc-bleed">

    <div>
      <p class="cc-kicker">Why now</p>
      <h2 class="cc-h2" style="margin-top:1rem">The crises of our time are, at root, crises of inner development.</h2>
      <p style="margin-top:1.5rem">Modern civilisation is breaking down in ways that cannot simply be repaired. The trouble stems from the deep assumptions, values and perspectives that shape our culture, and modernity has treated the inner world as irrelevant. We have no trusted institutions dedicated to large-scale inner growth for the common good, even as half a century of research shows how far people can develop given the right conditions. Developmental spaces are our answer to that gap.</p>
      <p style="margin-top:1.25rem"><a href="/why">Read the full argument</a></p>

<section class="cc-wrap cc-section">
  <h2 class="cc-h2">Five domains of growth</h2>
  <p class="cc-lede" style="margin-top:1rem">Held in parallel, not in sequence. A developmental space weaves them together in daily life.</p>
  <ol class="cc-domains">
    <li><span class="cc-num">i</span><strong>Cognitive development</strong><em>greater complexity of thought and understanding</em></li>
    <li><span class="cc-num">ii</span><strong>Awakening</strong><em>spiritual growth in the domain of consciousness</em></li>
    <li><span class="cc-num">iii</span><strong>Healing</strong><em>metabolising trauma; integrating unconscious shadows</em></li>
    <li><span class="cc-num">iv</span><strong>Evolving worldview</strong><em>circles of care widen toward a world-centric view</em></li>
    <li><span class="cc-num">v</span><strong>Engaged action</strong><em>inner shifts become ethical engagement with the world</em></li>
